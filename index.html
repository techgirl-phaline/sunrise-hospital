FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment online by clicking the "Book Appointment" button on our website, or by calling our hotline.

What are your visiting hours?

General visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. ICU visiting hours are restricted to immediate family members.

Does the hospital accept insurance?

Yes, we accept most major health insurance providers. Please bring your insurance card to registration.

Need Emergency Medical Help?

We are available 24/7. Call our emergency hotline or book an appointment instantly.
